Where AI takeoff still costs estimators time

A first quarterly review of AI assisted takeoff across 1,515 projects, the kind of report a first data hire ships in month one. It breaks takeoff accuracy, estimator review hours, and bid cycle time down by project type and trade, ranks project types by the review hours that errors actually cost, and sizes what targeted retraining plus a confidence gate would give back to the estimating team.

NOTEIllustrative data modeled on Bobyard's public product structure. Not a real customer's or the company's actual data. Built by Vishal Kumar as a candidate work sample, not an official Bobyard artifact.
